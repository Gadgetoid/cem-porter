ROM Explorer

Keep on keeping on!

Opens a Windows CE ROM image and shows what's in it, the way the device's own Explorer would.

Using it

  • Open an nk.bin, a raw flash dump, or a zip or 7z holding one. Archives with several ROMs list each in the toolbar.
  • Double-click a folder to open it, or a file to see its properties: version, CPU, sections, imports, exports and where it sits in the ROM.
  • Properties with nothing selected describes the ROM: CE version, CPU, address ranges and build dates.
  • Save the selected files, or everything as a zip. Programs and DLLs are rebuilt as ordinary PE files.

What it reads

  • CE BIN (B000FF) images and flat ROM or flash dumps, wherever the ROM sits in the dump, even wrapping round its end.
  • Windows CE 1.0 to 3.0, with their compressed modules and files.
  • Folders and shortcuts from the ROM's initobj.dat, so the tree matches the device after a cold boot.

Limitations

  • Relocations aren't in ROM modules, so rebuilt programs only load at their original address.
  • CE 4.0 and later compression, and Windows Mobile IMGFS partitions, aren't read yet.
  • Some devices keep their OS compressed in flash for a boot loader to unpack; those dumps have no ROM to show.
